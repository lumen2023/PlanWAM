PlanWAM: Planning-Shaped Future Representations for End-to-End Autonomous Driving

Anonymous authors

Paper under double-blind review

World models in end-to-end autonomous driving predict future scene evolution to provide foresight for trajectory planning. PlanWAM asks a more fundamental question: which future representation is actually useful for planning? We let planning objectives shape the future latent representation, then distill it into a history-only causal prior for deployable foresight.

01 / Overview

Overview

Existing driving world models mainly study how to predict the future and how to use it. PlanWAM instead studies which future information should be represented in the first place. The central idea is to shape the future latent with trajectory-planning objectives, so the model preserves information that directly matters for decisions.

A Temporal Register Pyramid compresses multi-frame observations into a compact, recency-aware history. A privileged future posterior learns a planning-oriented future latent from ground-truth future frames during training. Hindsight-to-Foresight Distillation then teaches a causal prior to predict that latent from history alone.

02 / Motivation

Motivation

Future modeling in driving has moved from explicit reconstruction to compact latent prediction and then to future-conditioned planning. PlanWAM keeps the useful foresight of these approaches while making the planning objective responsible for deciding what future information is retained.

A

Future information should be selective

A representation that reconstructs visual detail is not necessarily the representation that best supports trajectory decisions.

B

History should preserve dynamics

Historical compression should prioritize motion trends and interactions instead of treating every frame equally.

C

Foresight must be deployable

Future observations are available only during training, so the useful target must be distilled into a history-only prior.

03 / Method

Method

PlanWAM unifies historical modeling, future representation learning, and trajectory planning into a planning-oriented predictive representation framework.

01

Temporal Register Pyramid

Multi-frame, multi-view observations are compressed with a recency-aware query allocation. The four temporal levels use [4, 4, 8, 16] queries per camera, yielding a compact 128-token history representation. Recent frames receive more capacity because they carry stronger cues for immediate future reasoning.

02

Planning-Shaped Future Posterior

During training, the posterior observes future multi-view frames over a four-second horizon. Its future latent is shaped by trajectory generation and scoring objectives rather than generic feature reconstruction, producing a target representation aligned with the downstream planning task.

03

Hindsight-to-Foresight Distillation

A causal prior uses learnable future queries and historical context only. Latent alignment transfers the posterior's privileged future information into a history-only prediction that is available at deployment.

04

Foresight-Conditioned Planning

The predicted future latent is concatenated with the historical context and provided to both the trajectory planner and scorer. This lets foresight influence candidate generation and final trajectory selection.

Stage 1: privileged posterior L_task = L_plan + L_score + lambda_reg L_reg
Stage 2: causal prior L_train = L_task + lambda_latent L_latent
